# Frontend Design Skill Overview

This skill enables creation of **distinctive, production-grade frontend interfaces** that prioritize genuine design quality over generic aesthetics.

## Core Purpose

The skill guides developers in building web components, pages, and applications with exceptional attention to aesthetic details and creative choices, rather than defaulting to predictable patterns.

## Key Design Principles

**Before coding**, establish a bold aesthetic direction by considering:
- The interface's purpose and audience
- A distinctive tone (brutalist, maximalist, retro-futuristic, luxury, playful, etc.)
- Technical constraints
- A memorable differentiator — "What's the one thing someone will remember?"

**During implementation**, focus on:
- **Typography**: Distinctive, characterful fonts paired thoughtfully — avoid generic defaults
- **Color & Theme**: Cohesive palettes with dominant colors and sharp accents
- **Motion**: High-impact animations, often CSS-only, triggered by scroll or hover
- **Spatial Composition**: Unexpected layouts featuring asymmetry, overlap, and grid-breaking
- **Visual Atmosphere**: Textures, gradients, patterns, and shadows that match the aesthetic

## Critical Anti-Patterns

Avoid "generic AI slop":
- Overused font families (Inter, Poppins as defaults)
- Clichéd purple/blue gradients
- Predictable card-based layouts
- Cookie-cutter designs lacking context-specific character
- Designs that converge on common conventions rather than diverging meaningfully

## Execution Philosophy

Design complexity should match aesthetic vision — maximalist work requires elaborate code; minimalist designs demand precision and restraint. The emphasis is intentionality over intensity.
